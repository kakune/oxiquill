Math can be written directly in MDX. Use it near the prose and executable cells that depend on the notation so readers do not have to jump between pages.

## Inline Math

Inline math uses single dollar signs. In the logistic map, the state is $x_n \in [0, 1]$ and the parameter is $r \in [0, 4]$.

## Block Math

Block math uses double dollar signs:

$$
x_{n+1} = r x_n (1 - x_n)
$$

## Matrix

$$
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \\
4 & 5 & 6 \\
7 & 8 & 9
\end{bmatrix}
$$

## Aligned Equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= x^2 + 2x + 1 \\
     &= (x + 1)^2
\end{aligned}
$$

## With a Code Cell

Use an executable cell when readers should verify or vary a formula.

```python
#| id: math-python
#| title: Calculate a quadratic with Python
#| run: button
#| inputs:
#|   x: { type: number, label: x, min: -10, max: 10, step: 1, value: 2 }
y = x**2 + 2*x + 1
print(f"f({x}) = {y}")
```
